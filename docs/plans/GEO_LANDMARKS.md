# GEO LANDMARKS (round 1014, group D)

How a famous real building becomes 3D geometry on a GEO track, what shipped in round
1014 for the La Plata cathedral, which shipped set piece was parked, and what is still
missing. Modules: `td5_geo_landmarks.c` (which footprints are one landmark),
`td5_tg_city.c` (how the cluster is dressed, section "LANDMARK CLUSTERS"),
`td5_tg_prefab.c` (the shipped set-piece pool and its parked list).

## 1. Item 5: the parked set piece

**Which one.** `L23.lm17`, prefab index 17 in `td5_tg_prefab_data.h`: 327 vertices, 16
commands, footprint 11625 x 17883 raw, height 17367 raw. Decoded from the pick, not
guessed:

| fact in the pick | where it matches |
|---|---|
| `v327 c16` | `k_tg_prefabs[17]` is `{ "L23.lm17", ..., 327, k_pf17_c, 16, ... }` -- the only entry with that pair |
| pages `608 693 609 694 611 589` | `TD5_TG_PAGE_LM_BASE` = 559, and `k_pf17_c` names local pages 49, 134, 50, 52, 135, 30, 31 -> 608, 693, 609, 611, 694, 589, 590 |
| kind `building` | MESHTAG.BIN: set pieces are emitted as `TG_GK_BUILDING` |

**Which pool.** Two code paths draw from the 24 shipped Moscow set pieces, and the pick
came from the second one: the geo footprint fallback (`tg_geo_emit_landmark_prefab` ->
`tg_prefab_fit`) and the synthetic landmark walker (`tg_landmarks_place`, table row
"SET PIECE 17"), which still runs on a geo route over its built-up runs. La Plata's
race.log says `[PREFAB] 12 placed ... 4 site(s) refused for standing in a real square`
before this change and `11 placed` after.

**What was done.** `k_pf_parked[]` in `td5_tg_prefab.c`, consulted by
`tg_prefab_usable()`, which both paths already call. Nothing is deleted:
`td5_tg_prefab_data.h`, the real textures and `re/tools/td5_geomlib.py` are untouched, so
the piece can come back by deleting one row. Kept separate from `k_pfclose_exclude[]` on
purpose: that table is GENERATED by `re/tools/tg_prefab_audit.py` from a measurement and
rewritten on every audit run, so a hand decision stored there would be lost.
`TD5RE_TG_PREFAB_PARK=0` un-parks everything for an A/B or to look at the piece while it
is reworked.

**Scope: global, on purpose.** The geometry is incomplete wherever it stands, and the
J7 precedent (lm12, withdrawn from both users) is the same shape. Cost: any synthetic
seed whose walk used to place row 17 loses it. Seed 99991 slot 60 is unaffected
(MODELS.DAT 12982584 B, sha256 298DB07B..., byte-identical to the parent exe).

## 2. Item 20: what was wrong with the cathedral

### 2.1 What the pick actually is

`level091 e160 s23` is mesh 23 of MODELS entry 160 with centre (586211, 43950, -679963),
radius 33333.2, 106 vertices, 2 commands. That is **one of the two 110 m spires**, not a
generic block: OSM way 1450098107/8, `building:part=yes height=110 roof:shape=pyramidal
roof:height=30 roof:colour=Black`, 85 m2. It was drawn as a prism in the generic "city"
page family (pages 68 + 280 = house roof + a cream window-grid facade), which is why the
pick reads "city".

### 2.2 The cathedral is 26 OSM ways, not one

| | |
|---|---|
| outline | way 119950663, `building=cathedral`, 5574 m2, `height=20`, 182 points (decimated to 46), `wikidata=Q1050663` |
| parts inside it | 25 `building:part` ways: 2 x 110 m pyramidal spires, 1 x 90 m, 8 x 70 m gabled pinnacles, 2 x 60 m, 11 x 50 m gabled, 2 x 48 m, 1 x 47 m |
| roof tags | every part `roof:colour=Black`; `roof:shape` gabled / pyramidal; `roof:height` 7..30 m |
| wall tags | none (`material`/`colour` are on 17 of 90840 footprints in the whole cache, none here) |

So OSM already carries a usable 3D model of the cathedral. The map data was never the gap.

### 2.3 The three root causes (measured, not assumed)

1. **9 of 25 parts were never emitted.** A footprint binds to a route span only if one of
   its vertices is within `TD5_GEOB_BIND_MAX_B` (43000 raw = 100 m) of a route node. The
   cathedral outline's nearest vertex is 46.3 m from span 626, so it binds; the parts at
   the far (west) end are over 100 m out and silently fell into the "too far" count.
   Per-landmark log (`[GEO LM]` lines, new): 16 parts + the outline emitted of 26.
   The west half of the building was a bare 20 m box.
2. **All of it was dressed as an office block.** Wall page chosen by the generic
   `tg_facade_page_class` (glass curtain wall for anything tall), roof page 68 (house
   tile), UV rows = one per 3 m storey so a lancet window would be a slit. The brick
   cathedral read as a cluster of towers (see `log/f1_offE_span_626.png` in the test
   worktree: blue glass towers beside an office slab).
3. **No ground.** Nothing said "forecourt": the building stood on the same bare terrain
   page as the verges.

What was NOT the cause, checked with a temporary render probe (removed): culling. With
`TD5RE_RENDER_PROBE` the main outline mesh and the 110 m spire pass the sphere test
(`visible=1`) for the whole approach from 285000 raw out down to ~45000. The route passes
**abeam** of the cathedral at 46..100 m, so from the chase camera it sits 74..90 degrees
off axis, outside the FOV. It is visible from a fixed camera that looks at it and
fleetingly from the car when it turns toward the plaza. That is a camera fact, not a
generation bug, and it is the reason the framedumps for this item use
`TD5RE_CAM_FIXED`.

## 3. The general design: how a geo landmark gets 3D geometry

A landmark is a footprint OSM flags (`landmark_src`: worship 20, government office 9,
stadium 8, police 7, theatre 4, train station 3, ... of 74 non-part landmarks at La Plata)
and may own `building:part` ways (6 of the 74 do; 75 parts in the cache, 40 of them in no
landmark). Each tier below is tried in order and the first that applies wins:

| tier | source of the geometry | when it applies | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. OSM 3D** | the outline plus its parts, extruded with their own heights, roof shapes, roof colours | the landmark owns parts, or carries `height`/`levels`/`roof:shape` (`tg_geo_landmark_real`) | DONE; round 1014 added clusters, adoption and the kind palette |
| **B. dedicated model** | an authored mesh from a landmark library, fitted to the footprint | a `wikidata` / `name` match exists in the library | NOT BUILT (section 4) |
| **C. shipped set piece** | one of the 24 Moscow pieces that FITS INSIDE the footprint (`tg_prefab_fit`, biggest that fits, salted by way id), minus the parked ones | landmark has no 3D tags | DONE (round 1009 phase 5); the pool is the weak point: Moscow buildings, unnamed |
| **D. generic extrusion** | the real footprint at an estimated height with a facade page | nothing else fitted | DONE |

Everything in tiers A, C and D lands on the REAL footprint and the OSM-given orientation
because the vertices are the OSM ring in track coordinates; only tier C re-orients (to the
footprint's principal axis) and so has a 180 degree ambiguity.

Open space around a landmark is independent of the tier: the stand-down probes keep
procedural frontage off any real footprint, the plaza veto keeps small clutter out of a
mapped square, and the apron (round 1014) lays the paved surface.

## 4. What is still missing (decisions flagged)

1. **Wall material is a class decision, not data.** OSM has no `material`/`colour` on the
   cathedral, so brick comes from "it is a cathedral". A general mapping from `material` /
   `colour` / `roof:colour` / `roof:material` to a palette would cover the 17 + 38 tagged
   footprints cache-wide. Cheap, but needs a page-per-material table.
2. **Ring cap.** `TD5_GEOB_RING_MAX` is 48, so the 182-point outline is decimated to 46
   and buttresses, apse and transepts are lost. Raising it moves stack arrays in the
   emitters; the parts partly make up for it.
3. **Spire profile.** A pyramidal part is a prism with a pyramid on top (30 m on a 9 m
   square). The real spires are slender octagonal needles. A needle shape needs either a
   tapered stack of parts or a new roof kind.
4. **Facing.** Tier C has no notion of "front". The cathedral faces Plaza Moreno; nothing
   in the data says so. A rule "front = the side toward the nearest plaza polygon, else
   the nearest road" would orient prefab and library models and would also tell the apron
   which side deserves the steps.
5. **Tier B does not exist.** A library keyed by `wikidata` (Q1050663 here) holding an
   authored mesh in the `authored_fills.json` / MODELS command format, scaled to the
   footprint's principal-axis box, is the natural home for "the Cathedral looks like the
   Cathedral". The authoring pipeline exists (`re/tools/dump_landmark.py`,
   `docs/plans/LANDMARK_GEN_HANDOFF.md`, account3 authoring with web reference). What is
   missing is the loader that maps library id -> prefab data -> placement. Estimated
   ~400 lines in `td5_geo_landmarks.c` plus a data file under `re/assets/library/`
   (gitignored: needs a manual copy at merge, like the geo cache).
6. **Landmark pages are Moscow's.** The worship palette borrows three Moscow set-piece
   pages. They are positional (`TD5_TG_PAGE_LM_BASE` + local index): regenerate the prefab
   data and `tg_lm_worship_pages_fit` fails the build until the three indices are checked
   again against the page sheet. A La Plata-specific page set (brick, cement trim, slate)
   would remove the dependency.
7. **Visibility from the road.** The route passes the cathedral abeam, so only a driver
   who looks sideways sees it. Whether to bend the route or add a skyline billboard is a
   product decision, not a generation fix.
8. **22 vs 3.** 22 worship landmarks are in the cache; 3 are in bind range of this
   route. The adoption fix only helps a landmark that itself binds.

## 5. Reproducing

```
# build
cmd //c "<worktree>\td5mod\src\td5re\build_standalone.bat"

# fixed-camera framedump of the cathedral from the south, knobs on
pwsh -NoProfile -Command "& './verify/geo_f1_frames.ps1' -Arm after -Spans '626' -Prefix cat_on -Env @{TD5RE_CAM_FIXED='567000,19000,-715000,567000,14000,-671600'}"

# the same view with every round-1014 knob off (identical to the parent exe)
... -Env @{TD5RE_CAM_FIXED='...';TD5RE_GEO_LM_STYLE='0';TD5RE_GEO_LM_APRON='0';TD5RE_GEO_LM_ADOPT='0';TD5RE_GEO_LM_CLUSTER='0';TD5RE_TG_PREFAB_PARK='0'}
```

`TD5RE_CAM_FIXED` eye and target are in the world units the overlay `POS:` line prints.
The cathedral outline centre is (567106, ~10500, -671600); the east (route) side is x ~
590000, the route at span 626 is x ~ 609000. Read the PNGs: all of them are the same
byte size.

Decode any pick the same way: rebuild, dump `(entry, slot)` from MODELS.DAT with
`verify/r12geom_dumpmodels.py <MODELS.DAT> <span>`, match centre/radius/vertex/command
counts, read `MESHTAG.BIN[entry*256+slot]` for the kind.
